## Escalation triggers

If any of these happen, treat as urgent and update product within 48 hours:

1. **EU AI Act formal adoption of dilution deal** — update timeline references everywhere
2. **High-risk classification scope changes** — update out-of-scope detector
3. **Article 50 obligations expand or contract** — update Tier 1 disclosure templates
4. **ISO 42001 amendment published** — update Tier 2 ISO checklist template
5. **Major AI fine published** — update messaging (without leaning into fear)
6. **Competitor launches identical product** — update positioning + pricing if needed
