# Reuters · 7 May 2026 · EU Provisional AI Act Dilution Deal

**Captured:** 8 May 2026 (Day 0)  
**Status:** Provisional / not yet formally adopted

## What happened

According to Reuters (7 May 2026), EU lawmakers and member states reached a tentative provisional agreement to amend the EU AI Act, including:

1. **Delay of certain high-risk AI provisions** — from 2 August 2026 to **2 December 2027**
2. **Mandatory watermarking timeline** — would apply from **2 December 2026** if formally approved
3. **Various other dilutions** to obligations on certain providers / deployers (specifics still being negotiated)

---

## Why this matters for the product

### Implication 1: Don't anchor marketing on a single deadline
- The 2 August 2026 date for Article 50 transparency obligations remains official as of capture date
- However, broader high-risk timelines may shift
- Product positioning must rest on **ongoing governance needs** (procurement, vendor questionnaires, customer trust, enterprise sales gating) — not a single regulatory deadline

### Implication 2: Watermarking is a near-term opportunity
- If formally adopted, mandatory watermarking from December 2026 creates new template demand
- We should monitor this and add a watermarking-specific template to Tier 1 disclosures if it firms up

### Implication 3: High-risk verticals stay out-of-scope longer
- A delay on high-risk rules doesn't make them safer to serve — the regulatory uncertainty is HIGHER, not lower
- Out-of-scope detector remains essential
- Don't expand into regulated verticals based on a delay; expand based on ADVISOR coverage and proven safe templates

## What we don't know yet

- Exact text of the dilution provisions
- Whether the Council and Parliament will formally adopt the provisional deal
- Timeline for formal adoption (typically 2-6 months after provisional agreement)
- Whether any obligations will be tightened to compensate for delays
- Member state implementation variations
