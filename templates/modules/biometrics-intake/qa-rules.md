# QA Rules — Biometrics Expert-Review Intake

## Required framing

- Use readiness pack, evidence pack, review-ready drafts, intake summary, buyer/legal handoff, expert-review handoff, or questionnaire support language.
- Include not legal advice, not certification, and not a compliance guarantee.

## Forbidden claims

- Do not state that the customer satisfies a named law or framework.
- Do not claim certification, regulator approval, final audit status, or no further review.
- For expert-review-only modules, do not output final compliance conclusions.

## High-risk required line

Expert review required. Draft/intake only. Not legal advice. Not certification. Not compliance guarantee.
