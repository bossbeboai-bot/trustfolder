## You are interacting with an AI system

When you use {{ product_name }}, you are interacting with an artificial intelligence (AI) system, not a human. This notice is provided to comply with the transparency obligations of Article 50(1) of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).

## What the AI does

{{ product_name }} uses AI to {{ ai_function }}. The AI generates responses based on patterns learned from training data and the context you provide.

## What the AI does NOT do

- The AI does not have human judgment, emotions, or personal experience.
- The AI does not always produce accurate information. Outputs should be verified before relying on them for important decisions.
- The AI does not provide legal, medical, financial, or other professional advice.

## Underlying technology

{{ #if third_party_models }}
This AI assistant is powered by the following underlying AI models: {{ third_party_models }}. {{ company_name }} configures and deploys these models within {{ product_name }}; {{ company_name }} does not develop the underlying models.
{{ /if }}

## How your inputs are handled

{{ #if data_handling }}
{{ data_handling }}
{{ else }}
Your conversations with {{ product_name }} may be processed by {{ company_name }} and underlying AI providers to generate responses. Please review our Privacy Policy for full details on data handling, retention, and your rights.
{{ /if }}

## Your options

- You can stop interacting with the AI at any time.
- If you need human assistance, please contact {{ company_name }} support directly.
- If you believe the AI provided incorrect or harmful output, please report it via [contact channel].

## Where this notice must appear

For Article 50(1) compliance, this disclosure should be provided to users:
- At or before their first interaction with the AI assistant
- In a clear and distinguishable manner
- In a language understood by the user

## Standard implementation patterns

**Pattern A — Inline first-message disclosure (most common):**
> "Hi! I'm an AI assistant powered by {{ company_name }}. I can help you with {{ ai_function }}. I'm not a human, and my responses may not always be accurate. How can I help?"

**Pattern B — Pre-interaction modal:**
> A dismissable modal or banner before the chat opens: "{{ product_name }} is an AI assistant. By continuing, you acknowledge you're interacting with AI."

**Pattern C — Persistent UI label:**
> A visible "AI" badge or label next to the assistant's name throughout the conversation.

We recommend combining at least Pattern A with Pattern C.
